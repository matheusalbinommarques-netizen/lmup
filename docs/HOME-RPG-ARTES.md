# Artes da Home RPG

As duas ilustrações foram geradas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen, sem API paga configurada no aplicativo. São imagens independentes; nenhum texto, indicador ou painel da referência foi incorporado às imagens.

## Arquivos finais no projeto

- `static/art/home-rpg/vedria-traveler.webp`: vista com viajante, 1536 × 1024.
- `static/art/home-rpg/vedria-traveler-small.webp`: versão de 768 px para telas menores.
- `static/art/home-rpg/atlas.webp`: atlas decorativo, versão de 768 px.
- `static/art/home-rpg/atlas-small.webp`: versão de 384 px.
- `static/art/home-rpg/planning-small.webp`: derivado de 640 px do asset existente `art/bg/bg-missoes.webp`.
- `static/art/home-rpg/adventure-small.webp`: derivado de 640 px do asset existente `art/bg/bg-global-dungeon.webp`.

As imagens foram comprimidas em WebP localmente. As versões usadas pelo aplicativo ficam no repositório e no cache PWA. A aparência corporal do viajante é provisória e não representa equipamentos comprados. O companheiro desenhado sobre o cenário vem da seleção real do perfil. O atlas é ilustrativo: não promete locais jogáveis.

## Prompt da vista e do viajante

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production landscape illustration for the LMUP offline fantasy RPG home interface, NOT a UI screenshot. Create a breathtaking mature epic fantasy environment, wide landscape composition 3:2: a stone terrace beneath an old dark tree on the left overlooking a vast original medieval city of slender towers, ancient bridges and waterfalls nestled in a mountainous valley at sunset. A single anonymous traveler wearing a simple charcoal wool cloak sits on a low stone wall in the left foreground, seen from behind, human adult silhouette with face hidden, unarmored and unarmed, taking in the vista. This traveler is a neutral provisional character, not customized equipment. The traveler should occupy 35% of image height and be immediately recognizable, visible on the left third. Keep the right half a glorious deep vista. No companion, no pet, no dragon. Warm copper sunlight on city towers, desaturated midnight blue and deep evergreen shadows, realistic high-end painterly game concept art with fine stone and cloth texture, sophisticated atmosphere, no cartoon. Foreground terrace visually anchors feet and cloak. No text, letters, runes, logos, game UI, frames, numbers, watermark or interface elements. Render a standalone illustration only. Compose with room for small HTML greeting at top and HTML status band at bottom. Distinct original architecture, do not copy any game IP. 1536x1024 landscape.

## Prompt do atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original decorative atlas illustration, an image asset for an offline mature epic fantasy RPG home 'Mapa em preparação' card, not a UI mockup. Close overhead oblique view of a beautifully hand-painted parchment map on a dark stone and old oak desk, an intricate original mountain valley with a winding blue-green river, an ancient city of bridges and tall towers, forests and mountain ranges drawn in fine ink and muted earth pigments. A small brass compass and one candle near the edge, restrained warm copper candlelight against blue-black shadows, realistic sophisticated painterly game concept art, crisp material texture. The map does not represent playable locations, it is decorative concept artwork. Wide landscape framing with map dominating. No text, labels, letters, runes, symbols that look like UI buttons, interface frames, logos, watermark, characters or game screenshots. 1536x1024.
